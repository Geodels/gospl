**Deposit diffusion uses an upslope face diffusivity (2026-11-12).** The marine/lake diffusion (TS residual and Jacobian, Picard, provenance tracers) builds its operator in `hillslope._donorDiffCoeffs`: each face takes the `Cd` of its HIGHER cell. Do not switch it back to the Fortran `fctcoeff`/`jacobiancoeff` average `0.5*(ck+cn)`: with `Cd` set by deposit thickness, the average lets a bare cell shed bed into a deposit below it, the `>= 0` clip keeps only the gain, and the raw output carried ~2x its volume on steep bathymetry (the `_diffuseOcean` rescale then halved every deposit). The Picard operator also applies a **supply limiter** (`limit=(deposit at sub-step start, dt_sub)`: a cell's outgoing faces are scaled so it cannot export more than it holds; the ratio is synced owner -> ghost, a collective). Without it, at a large diffusion number (fine meshes) a deposit cell drains below its bed in one sub-step and the clip creates volume again (rescale 0.36-0.53 on a 10 km global run with the donor rule alone). The TS path has no limiter. The rescale is now a safety net that should print ~1 (with `picard`); a value far from 1 is a bug. A failed adaptive `ts` diffusion falls back to Picard for the rest of the run (sticky `marineSolver`). Guards: `test_marine_diffusion_bare_bed_does_not_shed`, `test_marine_picard_supply_limited`, `test_marine_ts_failure_falls_back_to_picard`.

## Dual lithology (coarse/fine sediment)
Opt-in, active **only** when stratigraphy is on (`stratNb > 0`). Enabled by `strata: dual: True` in YAML; the master flag is `self.stratLith`. **When off (default), every dual path is gated out and behaviour is byte-identical to the single-fraction code** — guarded by `test_dual_all_coarse_matches_single_fraction` (bitwise) and the regression suite. Full design + rationale: `docs/DESIGN_DUAL_LITHOLOGY.md`.

**State (all gated on `stratLith`):**
- `self.stratH` keeps its meaning = **total** layer thickness. `self.stratHf` = fine-fraction bulk thickness per layer (coarse = `stratH − stratHf`); invariant `0 ≤ stratHf ≤ stratH`.
- `self.phiS` = coarse porosity per layer, `self.phiF` = fine porosity per layer.
- `self.fineFrac` (per node) = `vSedF/vSed` of the routed flux (composition arriving at each node). `self.depoFineFrac` = the fraction `deposeStrat` actually uses; seeded to `fineFrac`, then refined inside pits (`_pitFineFraction`) and the marine domain (`_marineFineFraction`).
- `self.vSedF`/`self.vSedFLocal` = fine sediment flux (registered in `destroy_DMPlex`).
- Parsed params (`_extraStrata`): `phi0c/z0c` (coarse curve, defaults to `phi0s/z0s`), `phi0f/z0f` (fine), `fine_k_factor` (fine erodibility multiplier), `fine_diff_factor` (fine diffusivity multiplier), `bedrock_coarse_frac`, `pit_inlet_bias_*`, `fine_efficiency`.

**One shared hook does the lithology coupling:** `_surfaceComposition()` returns the per-node coarse fraction `fc` of the exposed top layer (all-coarse = 1.0 when off). Built on it: `_surfaceLithoK()` (= `fc + ff·fine_k_factor`, folded into `surfK` in all three SPL flavours) and `_surfaceLithoD()` (= `fc + ff·fine_diff_factor`, applied to `Cd` in `_hillSlope`/`_hillSlopeNL`/`soilSPL.diffuseSoil`). Both are 1.0 (no-op) when single-fraction or no contrast.

**Erosion** (`erodeStrat`) splits eroded solid into `thCoarse`/`thFine` by the consumed layers' composition (GROSS erosion; the SPL eroders then make them NET of their own in-place deposit via `_netRoutedSource`, see `docs/dev/FIXED_BUGS.md`; any new eroder that calls `erodeStrat` + `deposeStrat` and feeds `_getSedFlux` must do the same); the bedrock sentinel is inflated *proportionally to layer-0's fine ratio* so it never alters that layer's composition. **Transport**: `_getSedFlux` routes the TOTAL (`thCoarse+thFine`) into `vSed` and `thFine` into `vSedF` (same operator, exact). **Deposition is composition-only — the deposit geometry (`delta`/`dh`) is never modified, so elevations/flow are unchanged.** **Hillslope-creep deposits** get their `depoFineFrac` from `hillslope._hillslopeFineFraction` (creep-flux-weighted fine fraction of what the higher neighbours eroded; surface composition on the soil-creep path), called right before `deposeStrat`; any new process that deposits through `deposeStrat` must set `depoFineFrac` for its deposits too, or it inherits the stale routed value (fine was created under strong diffusion until 2026-11). `_pitFineFraction` (continental) and `_marineFineFraction` (marine) bias the deposit fine fraction toward the depocenter / deep-distal water by bathymetric depth, conserving the deposited fine volume; coarse stays proximal. **Compaction** (`_depthPorosityDual`) compacts each fraction on its own curve, conserving per-fraction solid. **Advection** (`stratalRecord`) advects the fine pile (`stratHf`,`phiF`) with a SECOND `strataonesed` call — a 3-fraction kernel would be wrong here (it would renormalise its extra 0–1 fraction fields, conflicting with the bulk-thickness representation). (The dormant `stratathreesed` kernel that embodied that approach was never called and was removed 2026-06 — see the "dead Fortran removal" note.)

**Fine-enriched overspill (implemented):** `_moveDownstream` threads the fine sub-volume through the cascade with **coarse-settles-first** retention — a filled pit keeps a coarse-enriched deposit (its `_pitRetFine`, used by `_pitFineFraction`) and the excess overspills fine-enriched, routed downstream and on to the marine domain. `vSedFLocal` accumulates the residual fine *mirroring* `vSedLocal` — **it is NOT zeroed in `_distributeSediment`; that was the bug in the first, reverted attempt.** Per-fraction conservation is guarded by **`test_dual_fine_conservation`** (`_fineEroded` vs `_fineDeposited`, the fine analogue of the total-cumED test, which does NOT catch a fine-only leak). **Any future change to the fine routing MUST keep that test green.**

## Several lithologies (`strata: lithologies:`, carbonates phase C0, 2026-11-24)

The dual fine fraction is **lithology 0** of a lithology table (`inputparser._readLithologies`: `litNb`, `litName`, `litFine` (fine-like or coarse-like), `litPhi0`/`litZ0`, `litK`, `litD`, `litBed`). An expert `strata: lithologies:` list (requires `dual: True`) appends entries. Coarse clastic is always the remainder of a layer. Design: `docs/DESIGN_CARBONATES.md` 2.7.

**State.** Piles `stratHL[l]` (bulk thickness) and porosities `phiL[l]`; `stratHf`/`phiF` are properties for lithology 0 (in-place writes work; bare `STRAMesh` objects get the one-lithology table lazily through `__getattr__`). Per-lithology eroded rates `thL[l]` (`erodeStrat`); `thFine` is the fine-group sum and `thCoarse` the coarse group (clastic + coarse-like). `litShare[l]` = share of lithology `l` in its size group at each deposit; `deposeStrat` lays `depo * depoFineFrac * share` (fine-like) or `depo * (1 - depoFineFrac) * share` (coarse-like), after `_groupShares` makes the fine-like shares sum to exactly 1. Budgets `_litEroded`/`_litDeposited` (and `_fineEroded`/`_fineDeposited` for the group). Outputs/restart `stratH_<name>`/`phi_<name>` (lithology 0 keeps `stratHf`/`phiF`).

**Dual lithology must stay bitwise identical.** Every multi-lithology path is gated on `litNb > 1` (`_litMulti` in `sedplex`, `hillslope`, `seaplex`, `iceplex`, `soilSPL`); the per-lithology loops in `stratplex` reduce to the dual arithmetic for one lithology (`_lsum`, `_litSoleFine`). The C0 refactor was checked on 13 dual variants (103 output files, 0 differences). Any change here: rerun that comparison (harness kept outside git, see `docs/DESIGN_CARBONATES.md`).

**Where each lithology's share is set** (every place that sets `depoFineFrac` must also set `litShare`):
- river routing: `_routeLithologies` routes each `thL[l]` (+ `_hillDelivL`) through `fMati` into `_litFlux`, shares from the routed fluxes (`_setLitShare`);
- pit cascade: each lithology is threaded like `vSedF` in `_moveDownstream`; a pit retains the same fraction of a lithology as of its group (coarse settles first, fine overspills); `_setPitLitShare` from `_pitRetL`;
- marine: the lithologies ride the provenance lockstep in `_distOcean` and the marine diffusion as extra columns (`_distOcean` reads its column count from the array). `_tracerComp` normalises the provenance block over its own sum and the lithology block over the routed TOTAL (they are only part of it); normalising both over the sum of all columns took the coarse clastic prior away and over-deposited a coarse-like lithology by 30-45 % (guard `test_lithology_split_matches_dual[coarse]`). `_marineLitShare` then balances the routed mix (iterative proportional fitting in factor form: per-node prior fractions, one factor per lithology, a 1e-6 blend of the domain mix so every node is feasible; the former in-place rescaling overflowed on subnormal priors and on nodes whose mix held only lithologies with no input, and the NaN shares collapsed a carbonate terrace run to a flat surface, 2026-11-24, guard `test_marine_balancing_degenerate_priors`); a node whose routed group mix is below `LITH_PRIOR_REL_FLOOR` (1 %) of the group volume it takes also starts from the domain mix, since that mix is round-off (it split a deep pelagic deposit by the partition, 2026-11-25, guard `test_carbonate_partition_invariant[resuspend]`) so each node keeps its depth-biased group volume and each lithology deposits its share of its group's input;
- gravity currents: each lithology rides its group's route as extra columns (`_cols(..., fineRoute)`), blended by `_gravLitShare`;
- hillslope creep and channel supply (`_litNodeShares`, `_litMix`, weighted by the donors' group volume), soil channel supply, glacial till (one abraded mix), gravity slope erosion (`thL` saved and restored like `thCoarse`/`thFine`).

**Guards:** `tests/test_lithologies.py` (table parsing; fine and coarse split into two identical lithologies reproduce the dual run and conserve each lithology, with gravity currents; np=1 vs np=3). Vectors `vSedLG`/`vSedLL` are in `destroy_DMPlex`.

## Carbonate production (`carbplex.CarbMesh`, `carbonate:`, phase C1, 2026-11-24)

Opt-in, needs `strata: dual: True` (raises otherwise) and marine deposition. `_readCarbonate` (called from `_readLithologies`) parses the factories (growth read in m/Myr, kept in m/yr) and adds the lithologies `carb_sand` (coarse-like), `carb_mud` (fine-like) and `reef` (coarse-like, only if a factory has framework) to the table (`carbLit` maps name to index), so `litNb > 1` and every multi-lithology path of C0 is active.
- `carbProduction` (before `seaChange`, inside the `seaDepo` block): on `lFill <= sealevel` nodes, integrates `dh/dt = sum_f P_f(sealevel - h)` with Heun sub-steps (`CARB_SUBSTEP_GROWTH`), capped at `sealevel - min_depth`. Framework thinner than 0.1 mm (the `deposeStrat` floor) joins the loose sand, otherwise the elevation would rise by an unrecorded deposit. The framework is laid with `depoFineFrac = 0`, `litShare[reef] = 1` (both restored after). Loose volumes in `_carbDet`/`_carbMud`/`_carbL`, consumed once by `seaChange` (added to the cascade input after the gravity split, mud into the fine flux, each part into its lithology column) and reset to `None`.
- `carbClasticRate` (after `seaChange`): the non-carbonate part of the marine deposit as a rate, the `sedrate` control of the next step.
- Open-water index (`exposure` control, phase C3): `_carbExposure` solves `(I - L^2 lap) E = A h - h` (for E itself, not the smoothed elevation: solver tolerance on the relief), `L = exposure_length` in metres so it does not depend on the resolution (the marine smoother's strength is in cells, which is why it was not reused). Cached operator/KSP `_carbMat`/`_ksp_carb` (fgmres + bjacobi; Richardson is too slow when L spans many cells), in `destroy_DMPlex`. Collective, but reached on every rank (gated on the configuration). Benchmark: parabolic ridge, `E = 2 b L^2` to 2e-6 away from the walls (the zero-flux walls perturb E by ~ 20 * 2bL^2 * exp(-d/L)).
- Pointwise apart from the budget reduction: partition-exact by construction. Guards: `tests/test_carbonate.py` (parse, budget: produced = framework + loose, reef exact, loose deposited minus open-edge export; responses; np=1 vs np=3), `benchmarks/test_carbonate.py` (keep-up/give-up threshold, drowning rate, step independence vs the exact ODE).
- Provenance with carbonates or pelagic rain: `_extraProvenance` (parsed after `_readLithologies`) adds one class after the user's, `provMarine = provUserNb` (`provNb = provUserNb + 1`); the source map is clipped to the user classes. The framework and the settled pelagic deposits go through `_deposeMarineProv` (`depoProvFrac` one-hot marine around `deposeStrat`, restored after); in `seaChange` the loose carbonate's provenance column is the marine class (`provFlux = provFrac*(sedFlux - carbIn)`, `+carbIn` in `provMarine`) and the resuspended pelagic sediment enters the marine diffusion's provenance in that class. Coastal-erosion material keeps the composition of the layers it was eroded from: `coastalErosion` keeps `provEro` right after its `erodeStrat` (`_coastProv`, per-class volumes, rescaled with the rubble conversion) and `seaChange` swaps `provFrac * coastIn` for it in the provenance flux (it took the river mix at the node, empty where no river arrives: a class eroded at the coast was credited with nothing). Guard `test_coastal_erosion_provenance` (its balance also needs the 1 nm `DEPOSIT_FLOOR`: with the former 1 mm floor the routed marine deposit discarded ~5 % of the thinly spread coastal-erosion material, fixed on dev 2026-11-25). Guard `test_carbonate_provenance_marine_class` (user classes balanced to 1e-3; they were 2 % off).
- Turbidity control (`{control: turbidity, length: L, source: transport|mouths}`, per factory, `carbFact[k]["turbidity"] = (L, source)`): `_carbTurbidity(L, source)` solves `(I - L^2 lap) c = q`, land identity rows, through `_carbPlumeSolve` (shared with the `solute` plume, cached `_ksp_plume`); read in `_carbStatic`. `source: transport` (default): `q = _turbRate`, the river mud carried through each cell by the previous step's marine routing: `seaChange` sets `_turbCol` (lithology-0 column of the `_distOcean` lockstep, after the provenance classes) for the FIRST routing pass only (not the Gmar re-routing), `_distOcean` sums the input and every pass's arriving column into `_turbThrough` (m3), and `_turbRate = _turbThrough / (larea dt)`; recorded only when a factory uses it (`_turbTransport`, parser). The cascade also carries mud through a few coastal cells that are not `sinkIDs`; the plume masks its source to `lFill <= sealevel`. Not restored on restart (the first step after a restart has no source). `source: mouths`: `vSedFLocal` at `lFill <= sealevel` this step. Why transport: on the mixed_carbonate_clastic margin the deltas carry the mud 30-40 km over shallow platforms beyond the mouths, where the factories grow; a mouth plume never reached them. Guards `test_carbonate_turbidity` (mouths, point source: volume kept to 1e-5, decay, response, bad source refused), `test_carbonate_turbidity_transport` (throughflow >= input and >= mud deposit per node, plume zero on land, nothing recorded without the response; fails without the in-loop sum), `test_carbonate_partition_invariant[turbidity]` (transport).
- Sea-surface temperature (phase C5): top-level `sst:` block parsed by `_readSST` (in `_readLithologies`, before the carbonate and pelagic parsers so they can check it); event maps loaded as local slices in `_initSST`, `_sst()` = event in force (step function) + curve interpolated in `tNow` (anomaly with events, absolute alone). Used by the `temperature` response (in `_carbStatic`) and the pelagic `temperature` table; output `sst`. Pointwise.
- Geochemistry link (phase C6): `_initGeoLink` checks the species and couplings (raises without `river_load`, or without `marine_coupling` for the supply). `carbSupplyLimit` (model: first in the `seaDepo` block, before the resuspended rain and the production) integrates the production once (`_carbIntegrate`, cached in `_carbProdCache` and reused by `carbProduction`) and computes the pelagic potential, then `_carbSupplyScale = min(1, yield * marineSolute[k] / demand)` (global sums: the same on every rank); `carbProduction` scales the production, `pelagicRain` the rain (when `pelagic: True`), and both call `_carbDrawdown` with the volume actually buried. `marineSolute` is written to every rank's output and restored on restart (it was not before; with the drawdown it is model memory). `_carbPlume`: river load of the species at the coastal exits (`marineSoluteInput > 0`, else marine nodes) per unit area, `(I - L^2 lap) c = q`, land identity rows holding their input. Tests `tests/test_carbgeo.py` (the yield of the test fixture's geochemistry must be ~1e-6 to limit: its solute inventory is ~1e15 per step in its units).
- **Resolved (2026-11-24):** the ~7e-4 m np=1 vs np=3 difference of carbonate runs with the soil model came from the soil SNES stopping on PETSc's step-size test (dev `a5c48ed`, `stol = 0`); the silica reservoir difference from `_solve_KSP(guess=False)` warm-starting from a stale Vec (dev `a5b8187`). Now 1.8e-6 m and an exact 0; `test_carbonate_supply_partition_invariant` runs with the soil model and compares every species.
- Known limitation: with provenance on, the loose carbonate takes the river composition of the node it is produced at (`provFrac * sedFlux`); offshore it falls back to the bedrock class at deposition. Provenance describes the clastic sources.

## Pelagic rain (`CarbMesh.pelagicRain`, `pelagic:`, phase C2, 2026-11-24)

`_readPelagic` (from `_readLithologies`) reads events sorted by `start` (step function, `_pelEvent`), rates in m/Myr kept in m/yr (maps: `[file, key]`, local slices loaded in `_initPelagic`), and adds `carb_mud` when there is carbonate rain and no carbonate block. `pelagicRain` runs after the `if not nodep` block, before the hillslopes: on `lFill <= sealevel` nodes, carbonate `R_c dt f(w)` (`_pelPreservedFraction`, linear lysocline to CCD) and clay `R_k dt`, capped at the water depth (the trimmed part is the `bypassed` budget item); laid with `depoFineFrac = 1` and `litShare` carb_mud/fine (restored after). Deposits below the `deposeStrat` floor accumulate in `_pelCarry` until they reach it, so small rates at small `dt` are not lost and elevation and stratigraphy stay consistent. Pointwise apart from the clay decay solve. Guards in `tests/test_carbonate.py` (`test_pelagic_*`, and the rain in `test_carbonate_partition_invariant[decay|resuspend]`).

**C2b.** `clay_decay` (per event): `_clayDecay` solves `(I - L^2 lap) c = 0` with land (`h >= sealevel`) as identity rows `c = 1` (`_buildDiffMat(fixed=land)`), rebuilt every call because the coast moves; cached solver `_ksp_clay` (fgmres, prefix `claydecay_`), `_clayMat`/`_ksp_clay` in `destroy_DMPlex`. Benchmark: 1-D strip, discrete `lambda^i` to 1e-10, `exp(-x/L)` to second order. `resuspend` (per event): `pelagicRain(resuspend=True)` runs before `carbProduction` in the `seaDepo` block, keeps the volumes in `_pelHand` and sets `_pelHanded` so the call after the marine step returns at once; `seaChange` adds them to `dh` after the 1 mm floor and before `_diffuseOcean`, into the carb_mud/fine lithology columns (provenance: the local routed mix, the bedrock class where none), and into the inputs of `_marineFineFraction`/`_marineLitShare` (`sedIn`, `fineFlux`, `casL`), so each lithology stays conserved. On the test mesh the diffusion gathers the mud into lows (thickest cell 13 times the settled one) and keeps 99.85 % of it (marine floors, open edges).

## Wave energy (`waveplex.WaveMesh`, `waves:`, phase C7, 2026-11-24)

`_readWaves` (called after `_readLithologies` in `_extraStrata`; independent of the strata, because the coastal erosion of C4 will need it without carbonates) normalises the climate weights. `waveEnergy()` solves, per direction, `(d . grad + kappa) E = 0` with the upwind coefficients of `getfacevelocity` + `advecupwind` (unit speed; computed once per direction in `_waveCoefficients`; `getfacevelocity` sets Fortran module state, which is why the coefficients are copied at once: the tectonic advection and the orography recompute theirs before use), land as identity rows with `E = 0`, the inflow edges of `_oroInflowBorders` (shared with the orography) with `E = 1`, `kappa = 1/decay + max(0, 1 - depth/shoal_depth)/shoal_length`. Matrix rebuilt every call (coast moves), solver cached (`_waveMat`/`_ksp_wave`, destroy list). Called from `_carbStatic` only when a factory uses the `wave` control (collective, reached on every rank: gated on the configuration). Global meshes (2026-11-24): bearings become tangent vectors from the local east/north (`_waveVelocity`), `zonal` belts by latitude or `from_map`; the inflow test uses each node's direction (`_waveInflow`); a fetch growth term `1/fetch` (diagonal and source) replaces the inflow edges a sphere does not have (required there). Benchmark adds the fetch recurrence from the inflow edge and behind an island (1e-14); tests add the global fixture and np=1 vs np=3 on the sphere. Refraction (phase C7b, `refraction: True`): `_waveRefract` turns the offshore direction towards the contour normal in water shallower than `shoal_depth` by `sin(theta) = sqrt(w/w_s) sin(theta_0)`; the normal is a least-squares plane fit of the depth in the local tangent basis (`_waveGradient`, synchronised owner to ghost), optionally of the depth smoothed over `refraction_length` (`_waveSmooth`, cached `_ksp_wavesm`/`_waveSmMat` in the destroy list); the coefficients are then rebuilt every step (`_waveBear` caches the bearings). Benchmark: planar beach, Snell to 2e-16. Benchmark: aligned strip exact `(1 + kappa dx)^(-i)` except the half cells of walls and outflow edge, island lee 0, weighted climate. Tests: parse, response, np=1 vs np=3.

## Coastal erosion (`WaveMesh.coastalErosion`, phase C4, 2026-11-24)

Runs first in the `seaDepo` block of the step (before the carbonate supply, the resuspended rain and the production). `waveEnergy()` is cached per step (`_waveT == tNow`): the erosion and the carbonate `wave` control share one solve. Erosion: marine `rate * E * exp(-w/depth_scale) * K`, cliffs (land within `cliff_height` above sea level with a sea neighbour) `rate * max(E of sea neighbours) * K`, at most to sea level (+1e-6 m), capped at `max_erosion`; `K = _surfaceK() * _surfaceLithoK()` with stratigraphy (1 without). The values are synchronised from owners to ghosts before use. With stratigraphy `erodeStrat` removes them (the river step's `thCoarse`/`thFine`/`provEro`/`thL` saved and restored) and the deposit-equivalent volumes (`_coastDet`, `_coastFine`, `_coastL`) are added in `seaChange` after the gravity split (with `rubble: <lithology>` the reef column is first moved to that lithology at equal solid volume, `_coastToRubble`; summary field `rubble_solid`), sharing the carbonate variables `carbMud`/`carbL` (both start at `None`). Benchmarks in `benchmarks/test_waves.py` (abrasion exact, cliff, lee); tests in `tests/test_waves.py` (parse, run, np=1 vs np=3).

## Karst dissolution (`CarbMesh.karstDissolution`, `karst:`, branch `feat/gravity-carbonates-karst`, 2026-11-25)
`_readKarst` (after `_readCoastalErosion` in `_extraStrata`) needs lithologies; default soluble set `reef`/`carb_sand`/`carb_mud` where defined, or `lithologies`. Runs right after the first `flowAccumulation` of the step and before the groundwater step (`model.runProcesses`), so `_routeRiverSolute` adds the same step's `_karstSolute` (m3/yr of solid times `solute_per_m3`) to the source of `_karstSp`. Rate `rate * R * max(Q / (R A0), 1)^focus * f_T(sst)` on land not under a lake (`_underLake`) or ice; demand capped by `max_dissolution` and halo-synced (owner values) before `STRAMesh.dissolveStrat`, which removes only the soluble piles layer by layer, leaves the insoluble rest and stops below `cover` m of residue (rank-local; provenance piles scaled with the layer bulk). Elevation, `cumED`, `EbLocal` updated like the coastal erosion; `karstDiss` cumulative (float64, written, restored on restart). Budget `karstBudget` = [bulk, solid per soluble lithology] (`--summary` record `karst`). The dissolved volume is NOT in `_litEroded` (it is not routed sediment). Tests `tests/test_karst.py`, restart case in `tests/test_restart.py`. Design `docs/DESIGN_KARST.md`.

## Marine routing surface (`seaplex._matOcean`, 2026-11)

Marine flow directions come from the smoothed bathymetry (`_hillSlope(smooth=2)`), eps-filled serially on rank 0 (`epsfill`), then `mfdrcvrs`. **`epsfill` only fills from seeds BELOW its cut-off and silently returns its input when there are none** (`fortran/AGENTS.md`). On a global mesh the cut-off floor is therefore the SMOOTHED surface's global minimum + 0.1 (smoothing raises the deepest cells, so the raw `hGlobal.min()` gave no seed, no fill, ~660 terminal sinks and km-thick force-deposits; fixed 2026-11-12, guard `test_marine_epsfill_seeded_on_global_mesh`); `sealevel + oFill` can still raise it. With a working fill the only terminal sinks are the seeds, so the unbounded force-deposit in `_distOcean` should stay negligible: a large `marine sink force-dep` thickness in zprobe means the fill failed again. **Dual lithology:** the marine fine fraction must stay conservative under clipping (`_conservingFineFraction`, bisection on the depth-proportional scale; guard `test_dual_fine_conservation`).

## Inlets of large depressions (`_identifyPitInlets`, 2026-11-24)

An inlet is a pit cell that receives flow from a cell outside the pit. It is found from OWNED donors only, counted per cell and summed onto the owner with an additive halo exchange (`localToGlobal(ADD_VALUES)` then `globalToLocal`), so ghosts carry the owner's answer. Reading a ghost's receivers (computed on its truncated neighbourhood) made the inlets, and so the spread of a large pit's deposit, depend on the partition. The per-pit `inlet_count` in `_diffuseLargePit` counts owned inlets only (a ghost would count twice). The method is collective and uses `tmp`/`tmpL`. Guard: `test_base_model_partition_exact`.

## Closed-sink deposit (`_closedDepo`)

Sediment reaching a flow-terminal cell that is not a pit (`lsink & pitIDs < 0`) and, since 2026-11-12, the volume trapped by a routing repair (`flowplex._pondRoutingRegion` -> `_keepRoutingPond` / `_sedFluxPond`) is collected in `_closedDepo` and deposited in place after the cascade. It is unbounded (zprobe reports it). Since 2026-11-12 it is handed to `_updateSinks` (`self._closedDelta`) and added to the pit/lake infill `delta`, so it goes through `deposeStrat` (layer, dual fine fraction from `depoFineFrac` = arriving `fineFrac`, provenance); before, it was added to `cumED`/`hGlobal` directly and left no layer. Guard: `test_closed_sink_deposit_recorded_in_stratigraphy`. The dual fine sub-flux at those cells is zeroed in `_moveDownstream`, so a closed deposit carries no fine. Snapping the ulp-above fill cells to `h` once routed whole flats into this path and broke `test_dual_fine_conservation`: keep flats draining through the pit machinery.

## Moving the stratigraphy laterally (`stratalRecord`, 2026-11)

`STRAMesh._stratFields()` is **the single list of per-layer fields** a lateral move must carry (`stratH`, `stratZ`, `phiS`, `stratK`, plus `stratHf`/`phiF` when dual, `stratDuri`, `stratCrustType`/`stratCrustSource`, `stratP` when provenance is on). **A new per-layer array MUST be added there**, or plate and scheme advection leave it behind while the rest of the column moves. `stratalRecord(indices, weights, onIDs, source=None)` interpolates every listed field (thickness-type and intensive fields with the same inverse-distance weights via `strataonesed`, crust codes by nearest source, provenance re-normalised to `stratH`) from `source` rows (default: the local layers; the advection paths pass rows fetched from their owners, `tectonics._fetchOwnerRows`) and syncs the halo from OWNED rows (`self.tmp.setArray(new[glIDs, k])`). Callers: `tectonics._advectStrati` (plate interpolation, once per plate event) and `tectonics._advectStratiScheme` (upwind/iioe, every step, semi-Lagrangian). The interpolation is not mass conservative on its own. Guards: `test_plate_advection_moves_stratigraphy`, `test_scheme_advection_moves_stratigraphy`, `test_plate_strata_partition_invariant`, the cyclic/sphere advection tests, `test_dual_lithology_advection_fine_pile`.

## In-model provenance tracers

**In-model provenance tracers (Approach B — functional, phases B0–B4 + B2b done).** Opt-in `provenance:` block (`inputparser._extraProvenance`, requires `stratNb>0`): sets `provOn`/`provNb`/`source_class` (the `cu_weight` copper-fertility weighting is NOT a model input — it is a post-processing arg to `gospl.analyse.provenance` `--cu-weights`, applied to the recorded `stratP`). State (allocated only when `provOn`, like dual lithology): `stratP[node, layer, class]` per-class layer thickness (Σ over classes == `stratH`, seeded to the bedrock `source_class` in `stratplex._initProvenance`); routed sub-flux `vSedP[c]` + `provFrac`/`depoProvFrac` + `_provEroded`/`_provDeposited` in `sedplex`; registered in `destroy_DMPlex`. Provenance is a **passive label** (no K/D/porosity/sorting feedback) — it rides the total-sediment routing. **Phases B0–B3 done** (functional end-to-end): B1 `erodeStrat` splits eroded sediment by class (`provEro`, Σ == total); B2 `_getSedFlux` routes each `vSedP[c]` through `fMati` (through-flux, Σ == `vSed`) → `provFrac`/`depoProvFrac`; B3 `deposeStrat` adds `depo·depoProvFrac` to `stratP` (keeps Σ over classes == `stratH`) + `_provDeposited`; a depositing node whose `depoProvFrac` sums to ~0 (no arriving composition — e.g. off-channel hillslope creep) falls back to the in-situ bedrock `source_class`, then EVERY depositing node is renormalised to sum to 1 so the layer is exactly partitioned (no `Σ_c stratP < stratH` holes → no `dominant==-1` cells in post-processing; `test_provenance_deposit_no_holes`). Guarded by `test_provenance_conservation` (single source ⇒ every layer stays 100% that class; class-0 leak == 0). **B4 done**: `stratalRecord` advects each `stratP[:,:,c]` like `stratHf` (re-normalised Σ==`stratH`); **`getCompaction` rescales `stratP` by the same per-layer ratio when it compacts `stratH`** (composition-neutral — only pore water leaves; without it `Σ_c stratP > stratH` after burial and the post-processed `stratP[c]/stratH` fraction exceeds 1; guarded by `test_provenance_compaction_rescale`); `_outputStrat` writes/`readData` restores `stratP` (lpoints, layers, classes) in the stratal HDF5 (`test_provenance_output_io`). **Multi-source validated** (`test_provenance_multisource`: 2-class map, both tracked, attribution spatially sensible). **B2b-marine done (now spatially-resolved, lockstep — 2026-06-26)**: `_distOcean` threads each class's incoming marine sub-flux (`provFrac·sedFlux`, Σ_c == `sedFlux`) through the **same** ocean cascade in lockstep with the total (shared clinoform capacity `marVol`; deposited/continuing fractions of a node's arriving flux carry its arriving composition unchanged — a passive label has no settling bias), producing a per-node deposited composition `self._marDepProv` (Σ_c == `vdep`). then the per-node deposit thickness is **diffused in lockstep** with the total by `hillslope._diffuseProvTracers` (the marine diffusion `_diffuseOcean` spreads the proximal clinoform deposit across the basin; since `Cd(h)` depends on the TOTAL deposited surface, every class diffuses through the SAME linear operator `M = I + dt_sub·L(Cd)` — built from `_donorDiffCoeffs`, advanced with `picardSub` backward-Euler **sub-steps** so the composition is not over-blurred relative to the adaptive total), giving `self._marDiffProv`. `seaplex._marineProvFraction` sets `depoProvFrac[marine]` to that post-diffusion mix (`_marDiffProv/Σ_c`, falling back to the pre-diffusion `_marDepProv`, then to the old domain-wide average `Σ provFrac·sedFlux/Σ sedFlux` only for nodes with no tracer at all), so a source confined to one margin's rivers stays confined to its offshore depocenter instead of being smeared ocean-wide. Tiny-flux guard (`arr > 1e-12`) avoids denormal 1/arr → inf → nan. This was the dominant sink (~79% of deposition) and the weakest spot of B2; conservation stays machine-exact (relerr ~9e-25 routing-only, ~3e-12 with diffusion), validated np=1-vs-np=4 (no deadlock; clean per-region separation frac0≈1.0 vs ≈2e-8; on `dual_lithology/input-provenance` the far-field domain-average volume fraction drops ~22-39% → 0.00%). The Gmar>0 two-pass case carries the pass-1 composition onto the `_depMarineSystem`-redistributed volume. New cached KSP `_ksp_provdiff` (`provdiff_` prefix, in `destroy_DMPlex`). **B2b-pit done**: `_distributeSediment` threads a per-class sub-flux `vSedP[c]·dt` through `_moveDownstream` in lockstep with the total — each pit retains its mix proportionally (`ret_prov = inVp·pitVol/inV`), overspills the rest through the same flow matrix (linear, so downstream-lake chains mix exactly), accumulating `_pitRetProv`; `_pitProvFraction` (from `_updateSinks`) sets `depoProvFrac[in_pit]` uniformly to `_pitRetProv/depo` (passive-label analogue of `_pitFineFraction`, **no depocenter bias** ⇒ simpler than dual). Σ over classes of `_pitRetProv` == retained volume ⇒ partition stays machine-exact. Guarded by `test_provenance_pit_fraction` + conservation tests (now run with pit attribution active). **B2b-hillslope done (2026-06-26)**: `hillslope._hillslopeProvFraction` (called between `erodeStrat` and `deposeStrat` in BOTH the linear `_hillSlope(smooth=0)` and non-linear `_hillSlopeNL` creep paths) sets the creep deposit's `depoProvFrac` to the **flux-weighted eroded composition of the higher neighbours** that fed it — `Σ_j a_ij·max(z_j−z_i,0)·c_j / Σ_j (…)`, `a_ij` the FV creep stencil weight (`sethillslopecoeff`, same operator as the solve), `c_j` the donor's `provEro` (normalised, ghost-synced). Creep is short-range so one hop suffices; fed nodes with no eroding upslope neighbour fall back to bedrock `source_class` via `deposeStrat`. Uses `tmp1`/`tmpL` scratch, NOT `tmp` (which holds Δh, read by erode/deposeStrat). Guard `test_provenance_hillslope_routing`. Plan: `docs/DESIGN_PROVENANCE.md` §6. (Bare `STRAMesh.__new__` stub tests don't set `provOn`, so `readStratLayers`/`erodeStrat`/`deposeStrat` read it via `getattr(..., False)`.) **Attribution is verified-faithful, not lossy (2026-06-26 investigation):** a report of "marine layers facing a source recording pure background class 0" was traced to **supply**, not the pipeline — recorded-in-marine source frac == delivered-to-sea source frac to ~5e-5 every step, eroded source (0.35%) == deposited source (0.36%), 99.2% of eroded source reaches the marine domain (not trapped on land), and the deposeStrat bedrock-fallback + submarine hillslope creep are each ~0.1% of submarine deposition. On that example the source rocks are tiny outcrops (49/71/84 verts, 0.4% of the domain combined; class 0 = 99.6%), so marine layers are ~99.6% background — a correct record of supply. **Do NOT "fix" this by fabricating source where none was delivered** (it would break the verified conservation). The visualization gotcha: colouring the wedge mesh by `dominant` shows class 0 wherever a source is a <50% minority (i.e. almost everywhere here) — to see a minority/trace source, colour by the per-class fraction `src_classN`, not `dominant`. To genuinely strengthen the signal, enlarge the source outcrops / raise their erodibility (an input change, not code).

## Channel cells in the hillslope diffusion (`channelQ`, 2026-11)

Optional: land cells whose discharge reaches `diffusion: channelQ` are held fixed by the linear and non-linear hillslope solves (identity rows / `h - hOld` residual rows); `_channelDiffusion` measures what the hillslopes deliver to each (positive part to `_hillDelivG`, a global Vec in `destroy_DMPlex`, added to the same step's routed source in `sedplex._getSedFlux` and then zeroed; dropped when `nodep`), and takes a negative part from the cell itself. With the option and deposition on, `Model.runProcesses` runs the hillslopes BETWEEN the river incision (and glacial till) and the routing (`hillslopeBeforeRouting`, which saves and restores the river step's `thCoarse`/`thFine`/`provEro` around the hillslope `erodeStrat`, the `glacialTill` pattern), so the supply is routed in the step it is produced (no lag; conservation tests close with nothing pending); without the option, or with `nodep`, the hillslopes stay after the deposition. The supply enters `sedChange` only, not the SPL coupled transport-limited deposition (`G > 0`), which sees the river step's erosion as before. Partition checks of a channel case need a threshold off the discharge lattice of a regular test mesh (multiples of cell area × rain): an exact tie flips cells by round-off and looks like a ~1 m partition bug (np=1 vs 2/3/4 at 1e-4 m otherwise; guard `test_channel_hillslope_partition_invariant`). The cached linear operator `_hillMat` is rebuilt when the mask changes (`_hill_chan`, reduced with `MPI.LOR` before the collective assembly). With stratigraphy, `_channelComposition` (between the hillslope `erodeStrat` and `deposeStrat`, scratch `tmpL`/`tmp1`, never `tmp`) converts the supply to SOLID volume (donors' eroded solid / bulk) and carries the donors' fine fraction (`_hillDelivFine`) and classes (`_hillDelivProv`) into `_getSedFlux`'s fine and provenance sources. The soil model is handled in `soilSPL._soilChannelSupply` (TS residual/Jacobian channel rows fixed via `_soilChan`; exact total, final-state split; fine from the donors' surface composition, provenance from the channel cell's class). Off by default (None): byte-identical behaviour.

## Marine gravity currents (`gravity:`, phase G1, 2026-11)

Opt-in (`gravOn`, parsed in `inputparser._readGravity`; off = byte-identical, every path gated in `seaplex.seaChange`). A fraction `f_h` of the river load arriving at marine nodes (`sedFlux`; `fineFlux` with dual lithology) is diverted before the clinoform cascade: fixed (`fraction`) or the analytic flood fraction `_gravFloodFraction` (log-normal daily discharge, rating exponent b, threshold `c_crit`; uses `QsL` = vSed and `FAL`). `_gravRoute` solves `(I - W^T diag(1-Lambda)) q = s` on a COPY of `dMat1` (column-scaled, so call it before `dMat1.destroy()`), with `Lambda` from `_gravLambda` (`1 - exp(-sqrt(area)/runout)`, times smooth TAPERS `clip((d - d_b)/d_b, 0, 1)` (water depth, `bypass_depth` default 50 m) and, optionally, `1/(1 + (S/S_b)^4)` (`cellslope` gradient, `bypass_slope`), synced owner -> ghost (hard switches made a speckled deposit: neighbouring cells with noisy gradients alternated deposit/bypass); 1 at terminal sinks (`is_sink_local`), 0 at `outletIDs` (export)) and an accommodation cap (room = `sealevel - bypass_depth - h` minus the cascade deposit; overfilling nodes get `Lambda = room/q`, total solve iterated, classes solved with the final `Lambda`; sinks exempt), through the shared `_solve_KSP(route=True)` (no new cached solver; a pond from a failed solve deposits in place). Scratch: `tmp`, `tmp1`, `tmpL` (free at that point). The deposit is added to `self.tmp` AFTER `_diffuseOcean` (not diffused the step it forms), then elevation/cumED/Eb/deposeStrat see the combined deposit; cumulative `gravDep` (numpy, output + restart). Dual lithology: coarse and fine routed separately (`runout`, `runout_fine`); the gravity fine is removed from the cascade's `fineFlux`, `_marineFineFraction(..., mdep=cascade deposit)` sets the cascade fraction and `_gravFineFraction` blends it volume-weighted with the gravity coarse/fine deposits (fine volume exact). Provenance (G2): each class supply is `provFrac` times the gravity supply (coarse and fine alike), routed as extra columns of the same `_gravRoute` solve (operator built once per run-out), and `_gravProvFraction` blends the cascade composition (set by `_marineProvFraction`) with the per-class gravity deposits (`_gravProvThick`). The `--summary` record carries `gravity` (supplied/deposited/exported) and `gospl-inspect` flags a `gravity_budget` that does not close. Budget `gravBudget` = (supplied, deposited, exported), closes to the KSP tolerance. Guards: `tests/test_gravity.py` (fine conservation and budget fail without the fine split, the blend, or `Lambda = 1` at sinks), `test_gravity_partition_invariant` (minimal_dual is not partition-exact on its own: compare after ONE step, `ab_partition --steps 1`; until 2026-11-23 the tool ran K-1 steps, so its default compared nothing). Design and phases: `docs/DESIGN_GRAVITY_CURRENTS.md`.

**G3b (2026-11-23): own network and slope erosion.** `_gravNetwork` sets `self._gW` (W^T) and `self._gSink` every step: `network: raw` (default) builds a matrix on the eps-filled RAW bathymetry (serial `epsfill` on rank 0 like `_matOcean`, `mfdrcvrs(receivers, flow_exp, ...)`), `smoothed` reuses `dMat1`; free it with `_gravRelease` before `dMat1.destroy()`. Receivers come from `_gravReceivers` (numpy: steepest `receivers` strictly-downhill neighbours, weights `S^flow_exp`, input-id tie break), NOT `mfdrcvrs`: that kernel uses equal weights `1/k` (so `flow_exp` did nothing), pads -1 without renormalising (flux lost), and its `k > receivers` branch flagged nodes with lower neighbours as sinks (sinks are uncapped: isolated mounds of 400 m). Strictly-downhill also avoids uphill receivers at pits below the fill cut-off (cycle, singular solve). Guard: `test_gravity_raw_network_conserves`. `_gravErosion` (only with `erosion_length`): `eps = (l/L_e) f(S)` with the depth taper, erode-or-deposit per node (`eps > Lambda` erodes, `Lambda = 0` there), `max_erosion` cap iterated with an LOR reduce. The eroded bulk goes through `erodeStrat` (river `thCoarse`/`thFine`/`provEro` saved and restored), and its coarse/fine/class parts are routed as an explicit source with `nodep` at eroding nodes. Elevation/cumED/Eb/`gravDep` get the NET change, `deposeStrat` gets the DEPOSITS only (`_gravDepoOnly`; the net double-counted the eroded fine). `gravBudget` = (supplied, deposited, exported, eroded). Channel-belt options (2026-11-23): `_gravNetwork` builds a second network `_gWF`/`_gSinkF` for the fine fraction when `receivers_fine`/`flow_exp_fine` differ (swapped into `_gW`/`_gSink` around the fine `_gravRoute`, both freed by `_gravRelease`); `_gravSmooth` (network_smooth, slope_smooth via `_gravSlope`) syncs owner -> ghost every pass; `_gravSpread` is a conservative SYMMETRIC thickness diffusion (do not go back to donor shares: they do not keep a uniform layer uniform on an irregular mesh, chains of dots); it syncs areas and degrees (a ghost's local FVarea is not the owner's: unsynced, the closure rose to 1e-6). `coarse_factor` keeps the old split bitwise when 1. Non-linear erosion: never iterate eps(Q) within a step (diverges). Guards: `test_gravity_channel_belt_options_conserve`, `test_gravity_spread_conserves_and_keeps_uniform` (red with donor shares), `test_gravity_coarse_factor_scales_coarse_supply`.

**Defaults, slope failure, benchmark (2026-11-23).** `coarse_factor` defaults to 0.3 and `deposit_spread` to two mean cell sizes when absent (`gravDepositSpread is None`; `0` turns it off). `_gravSpread(..., exclude=eroMask)`: eroding and failing cells receive no spread deposit (erode-or-deposit rule). `_gravFailure` (phase G3, `failure_slope`/`max_failure`): `dz = T_d min((S - S_f) l/2, max_failure, max(cumED, 0))` on the smoothed slope, zero at sinks/outlets, synced; its volume is added to `eroBulk`/`eroMask` and follows the slope-erosion path (one `erodeStrat`). `gravBudget` has five entries (supplied, deposited, exported, eroded incl. failed, failed); the summary record carries `failed`. Slope erosion warns in `SEAMesh.__init__` on cells coarser than `GRAVITY_EROSION_MAX_CELL` (collective allreduce, rank-0 print). Guards: `test_gravity_slope_failure`, `benchmarks/test_gravity.py`.

**Shared routing solver (2026-11-23).** `_gravSolve` uses the flow solver's cached routing KSP. It must never change the river routing: a supply that is zero on every rank returns `q = 0` without solving (a zero right-hand side counted as a failed solve and switched the sticky exact rescue `_ida_exact` on for the rest of the run), and `_ida_exact` is restored after every gravity solve. Partition testing: `minimal_dual` is not partition-exact upstream of the gravity step, and much less so on the ubuntu CI runners than on macOS (sediment load 8.6 % vs 5e-5 between np=1 and np=3), so test the gravity code with `test_gravity_operator_partition_exact` (identical input, exact to 1e-9) and bound the full-model deposit by the inherited load difference (`test_gravity_partition_invariant`). Guards: `test_gravity_does_not_switch_river_routing`.
